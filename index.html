Export film — renders your loaded track frame by frame, so every frame is perfect even at 2M grains, whatever your computer's speed.
Every particle is simulated on the GPU and settles into hidden figures: dancers, a kissing pair, wings, a hand. Each frame the particles are poured into a 3D grid and every pixel marches a ray through it, so they read as smoke: lit by a cold key light and by the fire, shadowed by themselves. The fire lives in the smoke's dense core, so you see it through the smoke in front of it.
Kick flashes the fire. Mids and bass make the smoke roll faster. Highs heat the fire. Quiet parts gather it in; a drop is a flash of light, never a blast.
Silent. It still breathes and drifts; give it music.
